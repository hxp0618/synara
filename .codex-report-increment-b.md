## Frozen design decisions applied

- D1: `minIdleUnits` is a non-negative floor inside `desiredIdleUnits`; PostgreSQL authority scope and SQLite safety
  triggers require the authority snapshot to match its Pool.
- D2: slot indices `[0, minIdleUnits)` are guaranteed plans and are applied before the cold Pod loop. Remaining plans
  retain their prior post-cold position. Pod naming and hashing still use the same plan objects in both reconcile passes.
- D3: only plans with `slot >= minIdleUnits` enter the `warm-pool-demand-fallback` candidate set. Other recycling and
  identity-safety deletion rules remain unchanged.
- D4: the target-wide `maxActivePods` budget still caps all creation. Truncating guaranteed plans is not a reconcile
  error; the published min/ready authority exposes the shortfall.
- D5: `synara_worker_pool_warm_deficit{capacity_class}` sums
  `max(0, min_idle_units - ready_idle_units)` per fresh row after bounded class collapse. Existing units also expose the
  bounded `desired_idle` and `min_idle` kinds.
- D6: no launch-transaction wait or retry was added. Placement keeps its existing cold-fallback behavior.
